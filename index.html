Matthew Fong

Gameplay Programmer

I am currently pursuing my Master of Science (MS) degree in game design and development (GDD) at RIT, expected May 2027, with a Bachelor of Science (BS) degree in GDD also at RIT. As my program covers a wide range of concepts, a better way of describing my skills and practices would be designing and programming rich interactive experiences.

My main area of focus is gameplay programming, however, 3D art and modeling has always been a field that intrigues me. As a developer, I am eager to continue growing, learn new concepts and challenge myself in both aspects of game design and game development.

I have programmed in software languages such as C++, C#, GDScript, and JavaScript. I am familiar with tools such as HTML5, CSS3, Sass, ES6, NodeJS, ReactJS, ExpressJS, REST, MVC framework, JQuery, Object-Oriented Programming, Websockets (socket.io), and Canvas.
I have also worked with various software programs, including Unreal Engine 5, Blender, Godot, Visual Studio, VS Code, Unity and Autodesk Maya.
