Devil Fruit V1

Overview

Inspired by One Piece's gomu gomu devil fruit, I created two variations of the devil fruit. Using primitives and bezier curves, I first modeled the shape of devil fruit. After some simple texturing and shading, I found myself investing most of my time in lighting and compositing (post processing) to really make my devil fruit pop and shine. Finally, with some simple animations, I rendered out a short video showcasing my irresistible devil fruits.

Tools Used

Blender

Duration

Less than 2 weeks

Team size

Solo Artist

Role: 3D Artist

  • Texturing and Shading
  • Compositing (Post processing)
  • Lighting
