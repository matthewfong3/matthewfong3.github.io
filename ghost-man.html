Ghost-Man

Overview

Ghost-Man is reverse Pac-Man but in 3D. The player plays as a Pac-Man ghost, Pinky, and must eat all 4 AI Pac-Men before they collect all the pellets or eat the player (when powered up from eating a power pellet). The player also has a top-down view minimap of the level, which allows them to constantly keep track of where they are and the other Pac-Men are so they can strategically maneuver around the level to either hunt them or avoid powered-up Pac-Men.

Tools used

Unreal Engine 5, Blueprints, Blender

Platform

Desktop Browser

Duration

Less than 3 weeks

Team size

2

Role: Gameplay Programmer

  • Programmed and optimized the placement of power pellets and regular pellets with instanced static meshes
  • Set up a top-down minimap overview with render target
  • Programmed the Ghost's (player) movement in blueprints
  • Programmed the hover animation for the ghost
  • Created and programmed the functionality for the menus (start menu, pause menu, game over and win screens) with widget blueprints

Role: 3D Artist

  • Modeled the ghost and Pac-Man models
  • Textured the ghost and Pac-Man assets
