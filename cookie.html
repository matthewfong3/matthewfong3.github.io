Cookie

Overview

I modeled and sculpted the shape of the cookie and the chocolate chips seperately. Then by using geometry nodes, I was able to randomize the placement of the chocolate chips onto the cookie. By using some random noise and height maps (normals), I was also able to make the cookie more realistic by adding in detailed cracks and bumps in the cookie dough. Finally, with some texturing, shading, animations, lighting and compositing (post processing), I successfully rendered out a short video displaying my grandiose cookie.
